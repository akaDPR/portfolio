# SSJ Website — The Two Options

Two finalist designs for Sri Swarnambigai Jewellers. Identical business content (copy,
products, weights, contacts, testimonials, offers) — opposite design identities. All 7
photographs appear in both. Older drafts live in `archive/`.

## Option A — `index.html` · "The Midnight Vault"

A night showroom. Gold under spotlight.

- **Identity:** warm obsidian black + champagne gold, Fraunces display serif + Jost,
  film-grain atmosphere, circular monogram
- **Opening:** gold preloader curtain lifts into a full-viewport hero with slow
  cinematic zoom and masked word-by-word headline
- **Signature moment:** "The Vault" — a pinned horizontal-scroll gallery of all seven
  pieces (desktop); swipeable snap rail (mobile)
- **Motion:** GSAP orchestrated intro, scroll drift, counters, parallax, magnetic CTAs
  on desktop pointers
- **Mobile:** snap rails for pieces and voices, 2×2 stats, bottom call/book bar,
  tap-press feedback everywhere

## Option B — `index5.html` · "The House Journal"

A printed magazine issue. Vol. 44, Coimbatore edition.

- **Identity:** ivory paper + ink + maroon, Italiana hairline masthead + Cormorant
  Garamond + Work Sans, paper-noise texture, heavy editorial rules
- **Opening:** newspaper furniture row, giant letter-by-letter masthead reveal,
  overlapping cover-collage plates with a rotating hallmark stamp
- **Signature moments:** numbered chapters (I–IV); lookbook plates with clip-path wipe
  reveals and written captions; "The Index" — an old-style price list where hovering a
  row previews the piece (desktop) and every row opens an enquiry pre-filled with that
  piece
- **Motion:** GSAP masthead assembly, editorial wipes, plate parallax, counters
- **Mobile:** collage-first cover, full-width plates, thumbnail index rows, letters as
  swipeable snap rail, bottom call/book bar, tap feedback

## The differentiation rule

| | A — Midnight Vault | B — House Journal |
|---|---|---|
| World | Night showroom | Printed magazine |
| Palette | Obsidian + champagne gold | Ivory + ink + maroon |
| Type | Fraunces + Jost | Italiana + Cormorant + Work Sans |
| Opening | Preloader curtain → cinematic cover | Masthead assembles like a front page |
| Gallery | Pinned horizontal vault | Numbered lookbook plates |
| Products | Dark cards | Price-index rows with live preview |
| Texture | Film grain | Paper noise + hairline rules |

Every future change goes to whichever version's world it belongs in, expressed in that
version's language. If it fits both, it's too generic for either.
