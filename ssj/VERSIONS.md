# SSJ Website — The Two Options

Two finalist designs for Sri Swarnambigai Jewellers. Same business content (copy, products,
weights, contacts, testimonials) in both — only the design identity differs. Older drafts
(`index2.html` dark obsidian, `index4.html` sepia heritage) live in `archive/`.

## Option A — `index.html` · "Daylight Boutique"

The warm, welcoming retail experience. How the store *feels when you walk in*.

- **Identity:** light cream + gold, soft rounded corners, centered section headers
- **Hero:** cinematic 4-slide crossfade gallery with synced headline word-morph
  ("story of trust" → "moment of forever" → …), arched photo frame
- **Motion:** gentle CSS reveals, shimmer headline, Ken Burns drift
- **Mood:** familial, warm, trustworthy — speaks to parents and families
- **Mobile:** text-first hero with in-flow gallery showcase, snap-scroll product and
  review rails, 2×2 trust grid, stacked full-width CTAs, bottom call/book bar,
  tap feedback on all interactive elements

## Option B — `index5.html` · "Maison Editorial"

The high-fashion magazine experience. How the brand *looks in a luxury editorial spread*.

- **Identity:** ivory paper + dominant maroon, sharp corners, hairline rules,
  numbered sections (01–04), thin double inset frame around the hero
- **Hero:** full-bleed photo inside the frame, oversized serif headline lower-left,
  featured-product card layered into the composition (product on screen from second one)
- **Motion:** GSAP + ScrollTrigger — masked word-by-word headline reveal, hero image
  scale-in and scroll drift, animated counters, story parallax
- **Mood:** premium, editorial, aspirational — speaks to bridal buyers comparing
  SSJ against big-brand showrooms
- **Mobile:** designed-for-mobile hero filling the viewport, product "polaroid" card
  overlapping the frame edge at a slight tilt, snap-scroll rails, tap feedback,
  bottom call/book bar

## The differentiation rule

Every future change must widen the gap, not narrow it:

| | Option A | Option B |
|---|---|---|
| Accent | Gold | Maroon |
| Corners | Rounded, soft | Sharp, hairline |
| Section heads | Centered | Left-aligned, numbered |
| Hero | Gallery crossfade, arched | Framed cover, product-forward |
| Motion | Gentle CSS | Cinematic GSAP |
| Personality | Warm shop | Fashion magazine |

If a change would make sense in *both* versions' style, it belongs in neither — pick the
version whose identity it strengthens and express it in that version's language.
