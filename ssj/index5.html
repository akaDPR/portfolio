The House

Forty-four years on Vysial Street — a short history of a long promise.

For 44 years, Sri Swarnambigai Jewellers has been a landmark of trust on Vysial Street — purity you can trust, designs you'll treasure.

What began as a small family store has grown into a destination for hallmark gold, antique pieces, bridal collections and fine silverware. Every ornament we craft carries the same promise we made in 1981 — and value that respects your family.
